The Codex, Grok CLI / Build and Grok Bot readers are adapted from the user's
AI Usage Widget v1.15.0, commit 25b305c:
https://github.com/wernerong/ai-usage-widget
Original files: source/Providers.cs and source/GrokBotProvider.cs.

AIHub.Usage is a UI-independent .NET library. It contains no WinForms or
Avalonia references. AI Hub owns its display, settings and notifications.
Grok Bot's adapter is Windows-only in this distribution.

The original floating widget remains independently installed. This version
does not alter its startup setting or replace its executable. Close the
floating widget when using AI Hub as the usage monitor to avoid duplicate
provider requests. The library can also be adopted by that application's
future releases; no cross-application live connection is claimed here.
