AI Hub v0.7.1 fixes flickering in hosted AI windows, including OpenAI / Codex.

When a workspace was shorter than an AI app's standalone minimum height, idle foreground maintenance could repeatedly expand and shrink its window. AI Hub now raises an already-fitted app without requesting another resize. Genuine resizing, split-pane focus, minimize/restore and pop-out continue to work.

Recovery-file saves now use the existing bounded retry helper when Windows briefly locks a file, while keeping replacement atomic.

Regression checks record intermediate resize events in both single and split panes, rather than checking only the settled window size. A read-only diagnostic can also measure live window geometry without reading conversations or sending input.

Validation: 286 automated checks and 58 interactive focus/minimize/restore checks passed. The regression fixture recorded 24 intermediate resize messages across 12 idle presentations before the fix and zero afterward, for single and split panes. A 10-second foreground observation of the same live OpenAI window recorded 56 bounds changes at heights of 780–900 pixels on v0.7.0, then zero changes at a stable 780 pixels on the corrected build.
