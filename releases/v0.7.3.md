AI Hub v0.7.3 fixes the remaining taskbar case that still needed another click to minimize.

A diagnostic of actual clicks showed Windows returning no foreground window at mouse-up, just before activating AI Hub. The previous handler cancelled the captured click during that brief gap. It now retains the click and waits for hub activation before minimizing. Releasing over another app or outside the taskbar still cancels it.

The user verified that one click minimizes and the next restores on their secondary monitor's taskbar. The diagnostic confirmed one minimize action for the completed click. Four regression checks replay the recorded event sequence and its cancellation cases; all 304 automated checks passed. Temporary diagnostic logging is excluded from this release. No computer-use automation or screenshots were used.
