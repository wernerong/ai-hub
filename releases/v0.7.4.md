# AI Hub v0.7.4

Closing AI Hub now closes its connected AI windows, including ChatGPT, Grok Bot, inactive tabs and both split panes. Each app receives its normal close request, preserving save prompts and close-to-tray behavior.

Pop out, Restore all, removing an AI and switching projects release windows to remain open independently. Update restarts and crash recovery also keep AI windows open. Provider processes are never force-terminated.

All 316 automated checks pass, including visible and hidden window closure, an external app process, cancelled closes, close-to-tray behavior, unrelated windows, stale process identities, recovery-file failures and crash recovery. No computer-use automation or screenshots are used.

Download **[AIHub-v0.7.4-windows-x64-Setup.exe](https://github.com/wernerong/ai-hub/releases/download/v0.7.4/AIHub-v0.7.4-windows-x64-Setup.exe)** for the normal per-user Windows installation. Existing installations can update from Settings. The ZIP is also available for portable use; checksums are in SHA256SUMS.txt.
