AI Hub v0.7.2 fixes taskbar clicks that only activated the workspace and needed another click to minimize it.

Click AI Hub's taskbar icon once while using a hosted AI to minimize the workspace. Click again to restore it. The handler now remembers the hosted app through Windows taskbar focus transitions and can complete a confirmed click even if focus has already returned to a visible AI pane. Completed gestures notify the hub immediately instead of relying only on its next timer tick.

Normal activation from an unrelated app, native hub minimization, either split pane, modified clicks, dragging and taskbar previews remain supported. The v0.7.1 flicker fix is retained.

Download **[AIHub-v0.7.2-windows-x64-Setup.exe](https://github.com/wernerong/ai-hub/releases/download/v0.7.2/AIHub-v0.7.2-windows-x64-Setup.exe)** and double-click it after closing AI Hub. Existing installations can also use the public release updater. Settings, saved projects, context shelf and provider conversations are preserved.

All 300 automated checks passed, including gesture-sequence regressions and the Windows self-test suite. No desktop input automation or screen capture is used for this release; an actual Explorer taskbar click has not been interactively verified.
